#### Exact differentials
The exact differentials correspond to state functions, which only depend on the initial and final states.

They are normally represented with "$d$" like in $dE$, $dS$ and $dF$, for the differential of the internal energy, the entropy and the Helmholtz free energy, respectively.

#### Inexact differentials
The inexact differentials do depend on the path and the process.

They are normally represented with "$\delta$" like in $\delta Q$ and $\delta W$, for the differential of the heat and the work, respectively.
