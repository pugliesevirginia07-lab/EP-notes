- Energy minimum principle: An isolated system at fixed energy maximizes its entropy to reach the lowest energy.
- Entropy maximum principle: A system at fixed entropy minimizes its energy to reach the highest entropy.

We assume that the entropy is not maximal, hence the nearby-state exists with the same energy but higher entropy. 

If the entropy changes, with the same entropy there exists a state with a lower energy. 

Therefore, the energy was not minimal and we have a contradiction. 

Finally, we can say that the maximum entropy principle is equivalent to the energy minimum principle.
