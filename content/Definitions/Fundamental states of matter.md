
(Or phases of matter)

### Solid

A solid has a fixed shape and a fixed volume. The particles are packed very closely together and held in place by strong intermolecular or interatomic forces.

They are difficult to compress and only vibrate around fixed equilibrium positions.

### Liquid

A liquid has a fixed volume, but no fixed shape. The particles are still close together, but with enough energy to slide past one another.

They are nearly incompressible and not locked in fixed positions.

### Gas

A gas has no fixed shape neither volume. The particles are very far apart compared with their size, with weak intermolecular forces.

They are easily compressed and can move freely (like independent particles).

### Plasma

A plasma appears when you continue heating a gas, the particles gain so much energy that the electrons are stripped from atoms. It results in positive ions and free electrons, this mixture would be a plasma.

They conduct electricity and respond strongly to electric and magnetic fields
