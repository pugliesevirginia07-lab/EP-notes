
A **phonon** is a quasiparticle (as if it were a particle) that represents a quantized unit of vibrational energy (like a "particle of sound or heat") in a periodic, elastic structure like a solid crystal lattice. They act as the **fundamental carriers of thermal and acoustic energy**, describing the travelling vibration.

The vibrations, described by the phonons, behave like a wave, but quantum mechanics says these waves come in packets (phonons).

*Debye* studies the heat of the phonons pretending a crystal is a box full of phonons, and calculating the thermal energy that they carry to get the heat capacity. At very low temperatures, $C \propto T^3$ .
- At high $T$, heat capacity is almost constant.
- At low $T$, there are not enough energetic phonons, hence the heat capacity is smaller.

Similar to the photons, we use the $E=hf$ using the relation with its frequency $f=\frac {c_s}{\lambda}$.
