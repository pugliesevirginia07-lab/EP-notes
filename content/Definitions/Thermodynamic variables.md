| $P$        | Pressure              | $Pa$            |
| $V$        | Volume                | $m^3$           |
| $N$        | Number of particles   | -               |
| $\mu$      | Chemical potential    | $\frac{J}{mol}$ |
| $Q$        | Heat                  | $J$             |
| $W$        | Work                  | $J$             |
| $F$        | Helmholtz free energy | $J$             |
| $H$        | Enthalpy              | $J$             |
| $G$        | Gibbs free energy     | $J$             |
| $C(T)$     | Heat capacity         | $\frac J K$     |
