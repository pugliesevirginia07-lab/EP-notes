
In nuclear reactions, a small amount of mass is converted to massive amount of energy ($E=mc^2$).

The total mass of the products is less than the mass of the reactants, with the lost mass (mass defect) releasing the energy. The mass loss is the change of the binding energy which holds the nucleus together, the more stable a nucleus is, more the excess binding energy that turns into kinetic energy or radiation.
