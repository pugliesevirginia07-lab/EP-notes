
A perpetual motion machine is a hypothetical machine that can operate forever without any external energy input or can produce more energy than it consumes.

Such machine is impossible because it would violate the laws of thermodynamics.

#### Perpetual motion of the first kind
Produces work without any energy input, violating the first law of thermodynamics (conservation of energy).

#### Perpetual motion of the second kind
Converts all heat from a single heat reservoir into useful work with 100% efficiency, violating the second law of thermodynamics.


Random thermal motion cannot produce continuous useful work (See [[Examples in thermodynamics]] *Feynman-Smoluchowski ratchet*)
