
The **DNA** is a double helix formed by two intertwined strands bound to each other. We assume that the fluid that surrounds the DNA is in equilibrium at temperature $T$.

The collisions of the water molecules with the DNA make it to deform and form a randomly coiled conformation, which fluctuates due to the Brownian motion.

Let us now fix the end points of our DNA molecule to be at distance R. The entropy will be inversed proportional to R.

![[Entropy of DNA.png]]

The two ends attract each other because the **most coiled conformation** has the **maximal entropy, hence the minimal energy**.
